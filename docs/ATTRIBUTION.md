# Attribution and preservation

The public name is **ACPedia — Revived by Celestro**. “Revived by Celestro” identifies
the restoration and hosting work, not authorship of the recovered encyclopedia.
The visible page subtitle links to Celestro.com, and every footer credits the
ACPedia/AC Community Wiki community and credited creators. The public
`Special:ACPediaCredits` page explains the source archives, surviving contributor
records, original artwork and software credits, and known gaps in upload/history
metadata. Existing copyright/footer notices, original article text, the recovered
logo, and the `AC_Community_Wiki`/`ACCWiki` namespace routes are preserved.

This restoration is based on the ACPedia/AC Community Wiki archives shared by the community-maintainer backup collection:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1IhL4mYC3P6bojeB005XTuhcYR5ApLC4q

- ACPedia text/history archive: June 15, 2021. Original article histories carry their contributors, edit comments and timestamps. Imported names are identified with the `acpedia-archive` prefix where appropriate, rather than represented as new local editors.
- Original images: February 17, 2018 base dump, plus a supplement covering subsequent uploads through January 14, 2024. The supplement README and its 37 File-page records provide additional provenance; the base image dump did not preserve complete uploader/time/description history.
- The recovered ACC Wiki logo `public/wiki.png` comes from `Wiki.png` in the original image backup. It is upstream artwork, excluded from the MIT code license.
- MediaWiki: https://www.mediawiki.org/ — GPL-2.0-or-later. Keep the upstream distribution's COPYING/CREDITS and extension notices.
- Variables and LabeledSectionTransclusion: Wikimedia project repositories at the commits in `sources.lock.json`. Keep their upstream licenses.

The old ACPedia siteinfo rights fields are empty; that is not a public-domain dedication. Preserve existing wiki attribution and confirm the original license terms for publication. Images, maps, screenshots, game art, logos and trademarks can have separate rights. No right to relicense all archive materials is claimed here.

Application/extension MIT licensing applies only to newly written restoration code, not recovered wikitext, artwork or upstream packages. Raw archives, generated databases and original source metadata stay separate from the tracked code. Preserve their checksums and original copies for future audits and corrections.
